---
title: All the screenshots in your documentation should be autogenerated
date: 2026-07-31
---

People that make complex GUI applications may have, over time, made screenshots
for bespoke tutorials to help say "look at this feature we have!"

This is nice, but if you are like me and are a small team, you may also have a
small library of examples in your head where you know your app can do X,Y, and Z
and you never had time to make a tutorial for it

To externalize this brain-only content, you need to make your entire
documentation system more automated

I recommend using AI agents to help you generate puppeteer scripts or other
similar automations to generate the screenshots for your docs. I also recommend
making your AI agent whip up a small 'review' portal where you can review
updated screenshots, and making it update only if e.g. 1% of pixels change just
for small tolerance.

There are many benefits to this type of workflow

1. These documentation screenshots exercise real high-value end-to-end user
   workflows that you want people to use
2. It forces that you work on accessibility, because you should make your tests
   use simple selectors, not complicated ones
3. It makes you think about navigating to complex states within your app, and
   you can then think: are there easier ways to get to this state in my app?
   Should you add url params that help navigate to this complicated state
   automatically rather than forcing users to go through laborious
   click-by-click workflow?
4. It makes your GUI documentation amenable to agentic improvement. I can just
   tell Claude "make a new tutorial for X" and voila its done. no clicking and
   pointing. I additionally tell it to "let the screenshots do the talking,
   absolute minimal prose"

There is no real excuse not to do this now if you use AI, and the benefits to
your documentation will be a huge net positive

This has been immensely transformative for my work on JBrowse I may expand on
this post over time, I just needed to spend 3 minutes to get this out of my
brain.
